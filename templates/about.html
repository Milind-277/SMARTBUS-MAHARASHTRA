About SmartBus Maharashtra

Simple, reliable bus booking across the state

What is SmartBus Maharashtra?

SmartBus Maharashtra is an online bus booking platform that covers destinations across all 36 districts of Maharashtra. Passengers can search for available buses between any two districts, choose their seats and pay conveniently through UPI, card, net banking or cash at counter.

How it works

🔍 Search Buses

Enter your origin, destination and travel date. Buses are shown with departure/arrival times, fare and available seats.

💺 Select Seats

View the interactive seat map. Select one or more available seats. Already-booked seats are clearly marked.

💳 Complete Payment

Choose your preferred payment method. Your booking is confirmed instantly after payment.

🎫 Manage Tickets

View and print your tickets from My Trips or My Tickets at any time — even after logging out and back in.

Coverage

SmartBus Maharashtra connects all 36 districts of the state including Mumbai, Pune, Nashik, Nagpur, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Kolhapur, Nanded, Amravati and more. Routes cover inter-district travel through major hubs across the state.

Important Note

The buses, routes and schedules listed on this platform are sample data used for demonstration. This is not affiliated with or a replacement for MSRTC or any official government transport authority.
